# Desktop gameplay

Mode: Operate. Build path: code-led. Authority: the user's supplied desktop gameplay redesign brief. No approved raster comp. The brief pins the visual world and supersedes concept alternatives.

## Form

A spatial tabletop with two facing creature rows, lands and supporting permanents behind each row, hand at the lower edge, and a right decision column. Use open space and card art to define the board. Desktop only: 1280×720, 1440×900, 1440×1080, 1920×1080 and larger.

## Own world

Warm near-monochrome charcoal, off-white text, restrained clay/vermilion action accent. DM Sans for interface typography. No heritage serif, felt, green/gold ornamental framing, glass, glow, dashboard panels, or invented marketing claims. Card art supplies the chromatic complexity.

## First viewport

A player can locate both life totals, the active turn and phase, their hand, combatants, stack and current required decision. The battlefield keeps its spatial continuity while stack and prompt share the right side. Exact legal targets remain selectable on the board and in an inspectable list. The primary commitment stays visible below scrolling choices.

## Visitor path

Public live game preview → email code sign-in → choose or import deck → launch constructed/Commander game. Deck editing, connection recovery, outcomes, spectator expansion and replay remain available. The backend remains authoritative for every action and choice.

## Signature interaction

Cards travel from their previous visible position to their destination. Target arcs link the source to exact selected objects or players. Combat links express assignments. Life changes get a brief signed delta. Hover or keyboard focus inspects card detail; motion never delays a required decision and reduced motion removes travel.

## Quality bar

A game-first desktop surface: clear state at a glance, exact command identity, readable prompts with visible progress, inspectable stack order, keyboard access, dense-hand reachability, no modal covering ordinary decisions. Evidence combines real gameplay and replay with clearly synthetic stress scenes. Card metadata fallbacks are deliberately included in gallery stress scenes.

## Verification boundary

The current pass covers gameplay and a cohesive shell theme, not new gameplay rules or a backend rewrite. True mana pool values are not currently projected by the server; the visible resource note honestly reports tapped lands. Visual proofs and native reference media are private local artifacts and are not bundled with the app.
